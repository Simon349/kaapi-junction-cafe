Filter coffee never went out of style — we just built an espresso bar next to it

Kaapi Junction started as a single steel counter run by two friends who couldn't agree on whether filter kaapi or espresso made a better cup. Six years on, we still haven't settled it, so we serve both — properly, without shortcuts, from beans grown on the same Coorg estates our families have bought from for years.

Every cup is pulled or decocted to order. No syrups pretending to be flavour, no machine left unattended.

Our Story

Two friends, one steel counter, and an argument about coffee that never really ended.

It started with a disagreement about froth

In 2019, Rohit and Meera opened a six-seat counter in Sector 29 with one espresso machine and one filter kaapi set-up, because neither of them would give up their preferred cup. Customers started ordering both just to settle the debate themselves.

That habit became the whole idea: Kaapi Junction never picked a side. We buy green beans directly from two family-run estates in Coorg, roast in small batches, and train every barista to pull espresso and decoct filter kaapi to the same standard — unhurried, no shortcuts, no flavour syrups standing in for real coffee.

Six years and three outlets later, the argument about which cup is better is still unresolved. We've stopped trying to win it and started just serving both, well.

What we stand by

Three things we don't compromise on, whichever counter you visit.

Direct-sourced beans

Every batch traces back to two estates in Coorg we've worked with since day one — no anonymous supplier bags.

Made to order

Nothing is batch-brewed and held. Your filter kaapi is decocted, and your espresso pulled, after you order — not before.

Trained baristas, not machines

We train every barista on both the filter and the espresso side before they're allowed behind the counter alone.
